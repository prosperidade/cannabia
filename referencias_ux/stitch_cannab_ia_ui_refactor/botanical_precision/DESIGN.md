# Design System Strategy: Biophilic Precision

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star

This design system is built upon the Creative North Star of **"The Botanical Laboratory."** 

In a medical/clinical context involving AI and nature, the UI must bridge the gap between high-tech clinical rigor and organic wellness. We avoid the "generic SaaS" look by rejecting rigid grids and harsh dividers. Instead, we embrace a "High-End Editorial" experience characterized by intentional asymmetry, layered translucency, and a focus on "breathing" layouts. 

The goal is to make the user feel like they are interacting with a premium, intelligent terminal inside a high-tech greenhouse. The interface doesn't just display data; it curates an atmosphere of trust, growth, and scientific precision.

## 2. Color Architecture & CSS Variables

The color logic follows a "Deep Forest" approach, moving from the darkness of the forest floor (backgrounds) to the luminous glow of new growth (actions).
